# Outreach log

Operational scaffolding. Where referral and press relationships

## Lost referrer hunt (OPEN)

Status: OPEN. Owner: Moncy.

The Apr 21 to May 18 2026 GA4 deep-dive found the referral
channel collapsed 81 percent. The prior period had 16 referral
sessions averaging roughly an hour of engagement. That is not
random traffic. Something embedded the site, linked it from a
forum or blog, or featured a track, and it went cold.

Find it:

1. GA4 -> Reports -> Acquisition -> Traffic acquisition.
2. Date range: Mar 24 to Apr 20 2026.
3. Primary dimension: Session source / medium.
4. Filter Session medium = referral.
5. Note the top sources by users and by average engagement time.

When found, log the source in the table below, attempt
re-engagement (comment-thank a blog, post a follow-up on a forum
or Reddit thread, ask about covering the new release), and record
the outcome. Mark this section CLOSED only after the source is
identified and one re-engagement attempt is logged.

## Monthly outreach review

On the first of each month, alongside docs/monthly-review.md:

- Pull the top 5 referral sources for the month.
- Anything new and worth thanking or pitching?
- Anything that went cold since last month? Re-engage it and log
  the attempt above.
- Any press or podcast that ran a feature? Log it and follow up.
