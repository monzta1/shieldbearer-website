About Shieldbearer

The Project, the Music, the Mission

#

Shieldbearer is the solo Christian metal project of Moncy Abraham. One man, no label, no gatekeeper, no genre cop and no list of approved tools.

Some songs, he straps on the guitar and sings them himself. Some songs, he hands the parts to AI. And when the off-the-shelf tools stop being fun, he builds his own.

The method changes. The message is fixed. Jesus Christ, named plainly, every song, every time.

#

Yes.

Shieldbearer is unapologetically Christian. The music exists to glorify Christ, proclaim truth, and create songs for those in spiritual battle.

What that belief means in full is the Creed.

#

In ancient warfare, a shieldbearer stood between danger and the warrior they served.

For us, that points to Christ.

Jesus is the true Shieldbearer: He bore the blows, stood in our place, and carried what we never could.

That truth is the foundation of everything this project is built on.

1 Samuel 17:7  •  Isaiah 53:4  •  Psalm 3:3  •  Psalm 28:7
#

Yes. Lanterns and the quieter releases carry the same conviction in a different register.

Songs like Still Be My Vision and Amazing Grace are built for the long night, for prayer, reflection, and the kind of strength that does not need distortion to be fierce.

#

You can listen straight from the source on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube.

If you want the full catalog in one place, go to The Armory. If you want the continuous stream, use Shieldbearer Radio.

#

Start by streaming the music, sharing it with someone who needs it, and wearing the message out loud.

If you want to go further, pick up merch, join the mission through the site, or support directly through Buy Me a Coffee. Every one of those actions helps keep the songs moving.

How It’s Made

Every Tool. No Permission.

#

When Moncy plays them, yes. Real guitars through real amps, Neural DSP, Bogren, or other modelers, chosen track by track.

Some songs carry AI guitar parts instead, or parts from the AI band he built. That call is his, song by song, the same way any producer decides what a track needs.

The question that matters is whether the track says what it needs to say.

#

Yes. AI vocals on most releases, AI parts wherever they serve the song, and AI tools he built himself.

Ask how any song was made. He’ll tell you. Then turn it up. The full method is on The Method page.

#

Moncy Abraham. Scripture comes first. He picks the passage, writes the lyric from it, and makes every call after that, including which tools build the sound.

The road behind him runs back more than twenty-five years through Bangalore stages, WhitenoiZ, Scarlet Robe, worship teams, and Dubai performances.

#

Tools Moncy built when the off-the-shelf ones got boring.

ToneCommand turns one plain sentence into a full guitar preset on a real modeler like the Fractal FM9. It is free and open source.

Bandsoff is his AI band. It writes and plays editable MIDI parts and mixes inside REAPER, on paid instrument and amp plugins. Both came out of this studio.

#

Because the method was never the gospel. Christ is.

David wrote psalms with a harp. Preachers used the printing press, radio, television, and the internet to carry the gospel. Every generation uses the tools available. The question was always whether the truth was being carried faithfully.

The Spirit moved over the void in Genesis 1. He has never once been constrained by the instruments available to the people He chose to use. God is not limited by your production software.

Talent is not the gospel. Tradition is not the gospel. Method is not the gospel. If the music lifts His name, the mission is accomplished. The full biblical case is on On Gatekeeping.

The full story is in the music. The doctrine is in the Creed. There is always a next step.
