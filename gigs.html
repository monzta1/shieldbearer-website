Live Dates

Gigs

Shieldbearer plays small, intentional live dates. DMV and Mid-Atlantic preferred. Solo electric set with backing tracks. Worship gatherings, church venues, college and seminary chapel events, small house shows, regional metal nights. Reach out below.

Where

DMV and Mid-Atlantic

Home base is in the DMV (DC, Maryland, Virginia). Available across the wider Mid-Atlantic on the right kind of date. Travel inside the corridor is straightforward; further out is on a case-by-case basis with appropriate notice.

  • Greater DC: Maryland, Virginia, DC proper
  • Mid-Atlantic: Pennsylvania, Delaware, New Jersey, North Carolina
  • Beyond the corridor: case by case, with travel covered

Read the Manifesto for why Shieldbearer plays live the way it does. Read the Creed for what gets said on stage. Both are settled before any inquiry comes in.

Format

What we bring

Solo set. Real electric guitar, vocals, backing tracks for the full band parts (real bass, real drums, real synths in the tracks). Roughly 30 to 60 minutes standard, longer for festival slots. Lyrics name Christ plainly. Theology is settled in advance and is not negotiable on stage.

Settings that fit:

  • Worship gatherings -- mid-week services, youth nights, conferences
  • Church venues -- sanctuary or fellowship hall sets, evening events
  • Seminary and college chapel events -- chapel slots, campus ministry nights
  • House shows -- small, intimate, real conversation after
  • Regional metal nights -- supporting slots welcomed, headlining considered
  • Outdoor festivals -- Christian metal / heavy worship lineups

Technical: bring own guitar, pedalboard, in-ear monitors, and backing-track playback rig. Venue provides PA, vocal mic, monitor wedge or aux send. Stage plot and tech rider available on request.
